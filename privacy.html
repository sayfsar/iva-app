Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Iva Assistant is a personal assistant used by a single person — its owner. It is not available to the public.

What data is accessed

With the owner's explicit consent through Google sign-in, Iva Assistant accesses the owner's own Google account data: Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Tasks, Google Drive, Google Docs and Google Sheets.

How it is used

The data is used only to perform tasks the owner asks for, such as summarizing mail, managing calendar events and reminders, and working with the owner's documents.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens and notes are stored on the owner's private server. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. To produce answers, relevant content may be processed by the AI model provider the owner has configured.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Iva Assistant's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
